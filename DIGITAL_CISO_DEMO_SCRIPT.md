## 🎬 Act 3: Cloud Provider Onboarding & Isolation

### Scene 4: Managing Cloud Connectors
* **[Screen Action]:** From the main navigation, click **"Cloud Providers"** (navigating to [`/providers`](file:///d:/security_platform/frontend/src/routes/providers.tsx)). Click the **"Connect Cloud"** (`+`) button to open the modal.
* **[What to Say]:**
  > *"Before we can evaluate security posture, we need complete asset visibility. On the Cloud Providers page, we manage our multi-cloud connectors.*
  >
  > *Connecting a cloud account takes less than two minutes:*
  > * * **Microsoft Azure:** We connect via an Entra ID App Registration using standard Directory and Subscription IDs.
  > * * **AWS:** We connect via a zero-credential IAM Role ARN using `sts:AssumeRole` with an external ID.
  > * * **Oracle Cloud (OCI):** We connect via Tenancy OCID, User OCID, and an API key.
  > * * **Oracle Fusion SaaS:** We connect directly to the ERP pod URL with read-only integration credentials.
  >
  > *I want to emphasize three critical guarantees:*
  > 1. ***100% Read-Only:*** *The platform operates exclusively in read-only inspection mode. It has zero capability to delete, shut down, or disrupt your production workloads.*
  > 2. ***Instant Handshake:*** *The moment you click connect, the system validates the credentials with the cloud provider's official API.*
  > 3. ***Military-Grade Encryption:*** *All credentials and private keys are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. They are never stored or exposed in plain text.*
  >
  > *Notice that each cloud account is assigned a unique identifier, ensuring complete data isolation between different business units or client environments."*

## 🎬 Act 5: The Security Command Center & Dashboard Analytics

### Scene 6: Central KPI Row & Scoring Formulas
* **[Screen Action]:** Click **"Dashboard"** in the sidebar (navigating to [`/dashboard`](file:///d:/security_platform/frontend/src/routes/dashboard.tsx)). Point to the top 4 KPI cards and the Speedometer Threat Gauge.
* **[What to Say]:**
  > *"This brings us to the heart of the platform: the **Security Command Center**.*
  >
  > *At the top, we see our four primary operational indicators:*
  > 1. ***Security Posture Score:*** *Currently sitting at 85%.*
  > 2. ***Connected Clouds:*** *Shows our active multi-cloud fleet.*
  > 3. ***Compliance Standards:*** *The active regulatory frameworks being audited.*
  > 4. ***Open Violations:*** *The exact count of active failures, broken down into Critical and High severity.*
  >
  > *Now, let’s explain how our two core scores work:*
  >
  > *First, the **Security Posture Score**: This is simply the percentage of evaluated checks that pass. If the platform tests 500 configurations across your clouds and 425 pass, your posture is 85%. When your engineering team fixes vulnerabilities, this score goes up. If new unhardened infrastructure is provisioned, it goes down.*
  >
  > *Second, look at the **Speedometer Gauge** on the right—our **Threat Index**: This measures your infrastructure’s active exploitability from an attacker's perspective. It is the inverse of your posture score, but with a critical difference: if our scans detect even a single open **Critical** vulnerability—such as an unauthenticated database exposed to the public internet—the system automatically injects an immediate **$+8$ exploitability penalty**.*
  >
  > *This guarantees that a dangerous, breachable flaw can never be hidden behind a good average score."*

* **[What to Say]:**

  >
  > *It highlights three vital metrics:*

  > *Below, the **Remediation Delta Checklist** provides an auditor-ready table showing state transitions from `FAIL` to `REMEDIATED`."*
